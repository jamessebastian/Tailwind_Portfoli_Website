Hello! I'm James Sebastian, an aspiring web developer who enjoys learning new technologies, solving problems, and creating meaningful digital experiences.

My Journey

My Background

I'm a technology enthusiast and web developer with an academic background in Computer Science Engineering and professional experience in WordPress development. My journey in technology began at Government Engineering College, Thrissur, where I completed my Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science Engineering from 2015 to 2019.

After graduating, I started my professional career as a WordPress Developer at QBurst, Trivandrum, where I worked from 2019 to 2021. This experience gave me the opportunity to apply my technical knowledge in a professional environment and develop my skills in web development.

Driven by my interest in expanding my technical expertise, I moved to Canada to pursue postgraduate studies at Humber College, Ontario. I completed a Postgraduate Diploma in Enterprise Software Development (2021–2022), followed by a Postgraduate Diploma in Cloud Computing (2022–2023).

My academic and professional journey has helped me develop a strong interest in web technologies, software development, and cloud computing. I enjoy exploring new technologies, learning continuously, and finding creative ways to solve technical challenges.
